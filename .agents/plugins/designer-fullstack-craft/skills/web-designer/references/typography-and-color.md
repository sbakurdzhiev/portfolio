# Typography and Color Systems

## Typography Pairing Strategies
- **Technical & Engineering**: `JetBrains Mono` or `Fira Code` for code, headers, and telemetry labels paired with `Inter` or `Geist` for body copy.
- **Modern Minimalist**: `Geist Sans` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` for clean, high-density interfaces.
- **Editorial High-End**: `Fraunces` or `Playfair Display` for display headlines paired with `Satoshi` or `General Sans`.

## The 60-30-10 Color Rule
- **60% Dominant Background/Surface**: Deep slate `#05080f` or dark tactical charcoal `#0a101d`.
- **30% Structural Elements**: Panels, borders, cards, muted text (`#1b2d4b`, `#64748b`, `#cbd5e1`).
- **10% High-Energy Accent**: Vibrant Cyber Emerald (`#00ff9d`) or Cyan (`#00d4ff`) for primary action targets and live state indicators.
